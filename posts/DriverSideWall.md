title: "Driver Side Wall"
date: 2026-10-29
description: "Building out the driver's side wall with birch shelving, hand-knotted shelf nets, and a butcher-block table that stows against the wall."
---

After finishing the van capsule interiors last month, I started visualizing how I wanted to build out the driver's side shelving. This would give us usable storage space while adding a nice interior finish to the van.

First, I removed the upper cabinets to open up the space.

![Starting the driver's side wall by removing the upper cabinets](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0631.jpg)

Then I added furring strips to create an outer box for the walls, starting with the lower strip and working around the rest of the opening.

![Lower furring strip in place](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0632.jpg)

![Outer wall framing fully boxed out](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0638.jpg)

From here, the process was very similar to the van capsule build. I started by building an inner wall box frame on the cutting table, then dry-fit it against the van's inner wall.

![Building the inner wall box frame on the cutting table](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0637.jpg)

![Dry-fitting the inner box frame against the van wall](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0642.jpg)

Unlike the capsules, this section didn't need a window opening. Instead, I attached a solid piece of 1/4-inch birch plywood to the inner box frame and backed it with a half batt of insulation.

![1/4-inch birch plywood backing for the inner wall box](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0640.jpg)

![Inner wall assembly on the bench before installation](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0641.jpg)

I mounted the assembly using Sikaflex and self-tapping metal screws, then filled the surrounding gaps with insulation.

![Inner wall box mounted in the van](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0648.jpg)

With the back in place, I built out the outer wall and added the lower shelf.

![Insulation added and the outer wall built out with a shelf](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0649.jpg)

At this point, I tested a few of the things we planned to store there.

![Testing storage items on the lower shelf](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0650.jpg)

I also wanted an upper shelf, so I added that along with the walls of the inner box.

![Upper shelf and inner box walls added](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0653.jpg)

Next came scribing the 1/4-inch birch inserts to cover the walls and shelves. Once each piece fit, I removed the individual pieces and applied polyurethane.

![Scribing the 1/4-inch birch lining pieces for the walls and shelves](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0654.jpg)

![Applying polyurethane to the individual birch pieces](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0656.jpg)

For the outer wall, I used the same approach as the van capsules: mount a sheet of 1/4-inch birch over the framing, then use the router to cut out the shelf openings.

![1/4-inch birch outer wall panel fitted over the shelving](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0657.jpg)

![Using the router to cut the shelf openings](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0659.jpg)

![Outer wall panel with the shelf openings routed out](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0660.jpg)

After coating the wall panel in polyurethane, I mounted it using #6 screws and brad nails.

![Polyurethane finish on the outer wall panel](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0663.jpg)

![First finished wall panel installed with screws and brad nails](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0664.jpg)

For the next section, directly behind the driver's seat, I followed the same basic sequence. First came the inner box framing.

![Inner box framing behind the driver's seat](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0684.jpg)

Then I added the rear panel, backed it with insulation, and attached the inner box assembly to the outer framing.

![Insulated rear panel and inner box attached to the outer framing](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0687.jpg)

I completed the sides and upper shelves with 3/4-inch birch. This section also got a bottle holder.

![3/4-inch birch sides and shelves, including a bottle holder](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0691.jpg)

As before, I added 1/4-inch lining pieces to the inside walls and shelves before fitting the outer 1/4-inch wall panel.

![1/4-inch birch lining pieces fitted inside the shelves](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0698.jpg)

![Fitting the outer 1/4-inch birch wall panel](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0704.jpg)

This part took longer than the first section. The corner of the wall had to be scribed to match the van precisely, so I used a compass and cardboard cutouts to work out the fit.

![Partway through routing the front wall panel](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0706.jpg)

Once the cutting and fitting were done, it was back to polyurethane, including the finger piece for the water bottle holder.

![Applying polyurethane to the front wall panel and bottle-holder finger piece](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0710.jpg)

The finished wall looked even better than the first one. All that practice was starting to show.

![Finished wall and shelving behind the driver's seat](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0711.jpg)

While I was building the wooden shelves, my wife was hand-weaving and knotting custom nets for them. These custom nets really tie all the work together.

![Close-up of my wife's hand-knotted shelf net](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0720.jpg)

![Custom shelf nets installed across the shelving](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0719.jpg)

We also wanted a table that could mount against the wall while we were driving, then come down for meals or for my son to do his homework.

That meant taking the upper wall panel off again and adding more furring strips to provide mounting points for the table setup.

![Additional furring strips installed for the table mounting points](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0722.jpg)

We purchased butcher block, cut it to size, and installed collapsible legs. French cleats provide the wall attachment.

![My wife installing the lower French cleat for the table](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0723.jpg)

Here is the table lowered into its usable position.

![Butcher-block table in the down position, ready for meals or homework](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0725.jpg)

Here is the final installed version of the van wall, with the table mounted against it.

![Finished driver's side wall with shelving, custom nets, and the table stowed against the wall](/images/Driver%20Wall/IMG_0729.jpg)
